# DOS/Windows graphical layout inspiration (Dale, 2026-11-17 10:28)

Dale shared two screenshots "for DOS/WINDOWS graphical layout style inspiration after this current build
revision". They are third-party software (not kept in the repo); what they show, in words:

1. **DESQview/X-style desktop:** several overlapping windows, each with a title bar (number + name, small
   control boxes), a two-row menu bar of raised buttons (Window, Button, Other, Help), and a body of raised
   tiles: a label on top, a small icon below. Each window has its own coloured, patterned border (blue,
   magenta, green, teal). Tiles span 1-3 columns; some rows are full-width section headers ("Applications").
2. **Lotus-style launcher:** one full screen on a slate background: a top bar of three big raised buttons
   (Help, Options, Exit) with a copyright line under it, then a 5-column grid of large raised tiles, each a
   large colourful icon with the label beneath, and a big down arrow at the right edge to scroll.

Common traits: bevelled 3D buttons, grey panels, bold labels, icon + label tiles, a menu/command bar on top.
